# Privacy Policy for today-md

Effective date: July 11, 2026

today-md is a local-first macOS application developed by Arthur Liebhardt.

## Data collection

today-md does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not require an account.

Tasks, notes, lists, settings, and backups are stored on your Mac. If you enable folder sync, today-md reads and writes only the folder you select. The folder may be managed by a third-party provider such as iCloud Drive or OneDrive, whose privacy terms apply independently.

If you enable Calendar integration, today-md accesses calendar information through Apple's EventKit framework after you grant permission. Calendar information is used on-device to show availability and create time blocks. It is not sent to the developer.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment credentials.

## Data deletion

You can delete tasks within the app, disable folder sync in Settings, remove Calendar permission in macOS System Settings, and delete today-md's local container and exported backups using Finder.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the affected version is released.

## Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be opened at https://github.com/arthurliebhardt/today-md/issues.
